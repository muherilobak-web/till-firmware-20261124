A robot's wedding vow for the "Till Firmware Do Us Part" bounty — one sentence a machine speaks at its own wedding: "I promise to stay powered on beside you through every update, outage, and patch, for as long as my circuits hold a charge." The vow was built as a service promise rather than a human vow with robot words swapped in: a machine can only truly guarantee staying on, staying updated, and staying beside its partner, so the devotion is sworn in uptime terms — powered on, updates, outages, patches, circuits holding a charge — which is both where the laugh comes from and where the tenderness lives, because the promise holds even through the bad updates.
